02 / Methodology

Two modalities. A richer experiment.

01 — Represent

A shared visual language

CLIP encodes claims and images into comparable vectors. Similarity can reveal alignment, but cannot establish factual truth.

02 — Fuse

Look beyond concatenation

Compare concatenated features with interaction features: absolute differences, elementwise products and cosine similarity.

03 — Compare

Account for group structure

Mixed Effects Random Forest adds group-level effects to a forest model. Its regression scores are experimental, not calibrated probabilities.
